3. How Your Data Is Processed

When you request résumé tailoring, job matching, or a text application package, the information needed for that request is sent over HTTPS to our Vercel serverless functions. The configured AI provider for that request may process the minimum résumé, profile, job-description, and instruction content needed to produce the requested output. Depending on server configuration, that provider may be Anthropic, an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, Cloudflare Workers AI, or DeepSeek. The browser does not call those providers directly. We do not use that content to train our own models. Provider-side retention or training, if any, is governed by that provider’s terms; this Policy does not claim those terms.

If you enable secure backup or use Job Agent features, confirmed profile facts, documents, job-search preferences, remembered application answers, application workspace records, and consent records are stored server-side in tenant-partitioned encrypted records using authenticated encryption (AES-256-GCM). Encryption keys remain in server-only production configuration. Missing or foreign ciphertext is not decrypted into another account. The Chrome extension does not keep a reusable copy of your applicant vault or résumé documents.

Confirmed information is what you save or explicitly confirm. AI-derived suggestions, match scores, and generated drafts are not confirmed facts until you review and accept them.

When the extension is enabled and you visit a supported Greenhouse page, it automatically reads the active listing's URL, job title, company, location, and job description so it can recognize the job and show its user-facing capture controls. That detected listing is held in Chrome session storage. It is not sent to 1stStep.ai merely because you visited the page.

Assisted employer-form fill and employer transmission are not part of this controlled beta. Software for those steps may exist and remain unused. 1stStep will not transmit your personal data to an employer unless a future, separately authorized feature is enabled and you give a separate action-time confirmation for that exact action. The extension does not click the employer's final submit control.

5. Data Retention

Encrypted applicant-vault records (including confirmed facts and remembered application answers), consent records, preferences, and schedules may be retained for up to 365 days after their most recent use. Application-workspace records, generated-document objects if created, notifications, and related workflow records generally expire within 30 to 90 days. Content-free operational and spend measurements generally expire within 8 days, and signed extension handoff tokens expire within minutes. Private package-file object storage and PDF/DOCX rendering are not launch requirements for this invite; if those systems are unconfigured, those objects are not created.

The extension keeps the currently detected job in Chrome session storage for the browser session and may keep a local capture handoff for up to 24 hours while delivery is pending. When you are signed in, have accepted the current Job Agent data-consent terms, and use an account-backed capture path, the captured job is saved to your account as a durable record containing its title, employer, location, job URL, description text, capture method, and listing-verification status. That account record is encrypted at rest, scoped to your account, and expires automatically 90 days after capture. It is not deleted when you close the tab, sign out, or uninstall the extension. You can export or delete these account records as described in Section 6, and account deletion removes them. A Resume Builder handoff without the required account consent does not create that durable account record; its local copy remains available until acknowledged delivery, local expiry and cleanup, clearing extension data, or uninstalling the extension.

Revoking an individual saved fact prevents its future use but may retain its prior encrypted version until the containing vault expires or the account is deleted. An account-deletion request removes the active tenant Job Agent records controlled by 1stStep (vault, consent, captured jobs, runs, workspace records, learning state, and related operational keys), subject to provider processing time and records that must be retained for security, payment, tax, dispute, or other legal obligations. Some payment and email-suppression records are maintained by the applicable provider under its own retention requirements. Uninstalling the extension does not delete server-side account records.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

1stStep.ai uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie for signed-in access and may use browser localStorage or sessionStorage for device-local workflow state. Information is transmitted to our servers when you request cloud backup, matching, tailoring, extension capture handoff, text application preparation, export, deletion, or another server-backed feature.

The Chrome extension uses Chrome session storage for the currently detected job, plus a five-minute cache containing whether Job Agent access is available, the account tier, and the access-expiry time so the popup does not need to query the app every time it opens. Chrome local storage holds a pending capture handoff. With a signed-in account and current Job Agent data consent, an account-backed capture is additionally saved server-side for up to 90 days, as described in Section 5. Without that authorization, the Resume Builder handoff remains local and no durable captured-job account record is created. The extension authenticates server requests through the open app.1ststep.ai tab and its HTTP-only session cookie; it does not read or store that cookie, a bearer token, passwords, complete applicant-vault records, or reusable résumé documents. Stripe may set cookies for fraud prevention and payment processing.

8. Chrome Extension

This beta’s intended purpose. In this controlled beta, the extension’s intended purpose is to help a user capture a job listing they are already viewing and send it to the 1stStep.ai Resume Builder or Job Agent review workspace. It is not a general browsing monitor, credential manager, or application-submission tool. Assisted employer-form fill is not part of this invite.

Complete functional outline for this invite. The extension:

  • Opens the 1stStep.ai setup page after installation so the user can sign in and configure the Job Agent.
  • Detects the current listing locally on declared Greenhouse pages while enabled, rechecking after single-page navigation changes, and shows a non-blocking shortcut to open the job in 1stStep.ai. On other pages, it reads only after you invoke it. Tuned extraction supports Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, and SmartRecruiters, with a general fallback for visible listing content or text you highlight.
  • Shows the detected job in the extension interface and lets the user correct a missing title or company.
  • Opens the detected job in the 1stStep.ai Resume Builder or Job Agent review workspace.
  • Checks through an open 1stStep.ai tab whether the user has an authenticated session and access to Job Agent features. The extension does not read or store the HTTP-only session cookie.
  • Uses a unique capture identifier to deliver a selected job to the correct 1stStep.ai page. With a signed-in account and current Job Agent data consent, the captured job is saved to your account as an encrypted record that expires after 90 days; otherwise the local handoff remains available for up to 24 hours while delivery is pending.

Fill software that is not part of this invite. The installed extension may still contain code that can scan ordinary fields on specifically supported application pages. That fill path is not enabled as part of this controlled beta and does not submit applications. 1stStep will not use it to transmit personal data to an employer unless a future, separately authorized feature is turned on and you give a separate action-time confirmation for that exact action.

When it operates. The extension loads automatically only on app.1ststep.ai and Greenhouse pages, its sole declared host permissions. It does not run in the background on other sites and does not watch pages you have not acted on. Website content is transmitted to 1stStep.ai only for a user-facing capture action that you initiate, such as saving the job to your account.

9. Data Security & Breach Notification

We use HTTPS for data in transit, authenticated encryption for protected server-side records, tenant-scoped access controls, revocable sessions, bounded access tokens, rate limits, and server-only provider credentials. Passwords, OTPs, CAPTCHA answers, and full payment card numbers are not stored by 1stStep. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the event of a security breach affecting personal information as defined by the New Jersey Identity Theft Prevention Act (N.J.S.A. 56:8-163), we will notify affected New Jersey residents as that statute requires: in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay, consistent with the legitimate needs of law enforcement and measures needed to determine the scope of the breach and restore reasonable integrity of the data system. Advance notice to the New Jersey State Police is required before customer notice under that statute.
