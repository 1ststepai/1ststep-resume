# Draft Chrome Web Store listing

## 1stStep.ai Job Agent

Save a job posting to My Jobs in one click—without copying and pasting the description. 1stStep captures the job details, opens your Job Agent, and prepares the next steps in your application workflow.

On supported application pages, the extension can also attach your approved résumé and fill ordinary, verified fields. It pauses for passwords, one-time codes, CAPTCHAs, identity checks, sensitive questions, unexpected fields, and final submission.

The extension does not store your profile, résumé, sign-in token, employer answers, or application status. It never claims an application was submitted without a separately verified employer receipt.

Requires a 1stStep.ai Job Agent account and an open signed-in `app.1ststep.ai` tab.
