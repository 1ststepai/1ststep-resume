A few things worth knowing.

How the agent helps

It checks roles, prepares documents from your confirmed experience, and puts missing answers in one place. You review and submit applications yourself.

Job Agent or Résumé Builder?

Use the Job Agent for your search, or the Résumé Builder when you already have a role in mind. Both keep you in control.

Your information and your control

Questions

Straight answers.

Will it apply for me without asking?
No. Your agent prepares the materials. You review them and submit the application yourself.
Does it need my passwords?
No. Employer passwords are never asked for or kept as reusable facts. If a site makes you sign in, that step is handed to you.
What happens at a security code or CAPTCHA?
It stops and puts the step in your list. It does not try to solve CAPTCHAs, and it does not store one-time codes.
Will the résumé sound like a robot wrote it?
It is built from facts you confirmed and written for that specific job. Nothing is invented, and the output stays plain and ATS-safe rather than padded with buzzwords.
How does it pick jobs?
Against the criteria and confirmed facts you set. Roles under your stated bar are filtered out rather than counted toward a quota. Protected characteristics are never used for ranking.
How are applications counted?
Only when the employer confirms receipt. Written, ready, and waiting on you are tracked separately and never counted as submitted.
How do I actually send the application?
Review your prepared résumé and cover letter, then submit them on the employer’s website. 1stStep does not currently submit applications on your behalf.
What does it cost?
1stStep Complete will be $39/month when paid access opens. One membership includes the resume builder, cover-letter tools, tracker, extension workflow, and Job Agent. Early access is free by invitation, and there is no paid checkout today. Any later subscription requires your separate agreement.
Can I stop it?
Yes, at any time. You can pause a run, edit or delete confirmed facts, withdraw permission, and ask for your data to be deleted.
