Questions

Straight answers.

Will it apply for me without asking?
No. Your agent prepares the materials. You review them and submit the application yourself. 1stStep does not currently submit applications on your behalf, and you can pause a run or withdraw permission at any time.
What happens at a sign-in, security code, or CAPTCHA?
That step is handed to you. Employer passwords are never kept as reusable facts, one-time codes are not stored, and the agent does not try to solve CAPTCHAs.
Will the résumé sound like a robot wrote it?
It is built from facts you confirmed and written for that specific job. Nothing is invented, and the output stays plain and ATS-safe rather than padded with buzzwords.
How does it pick and count jobs?
It checks the criteria and confirmed facts you set. Roles under your stated bar are filtered out. An application counts as submitted only when the employer confirms receipt.
Does the waitlist give me Job Agent beta access?
No. The waitlist captures demand only. Controlled-beta access stays limited to explicitly invited members and is checked separately after you sign in. Joining the waitlist never adds you to the invited-tenant list.
What does it cost?
1stStep Complete will be $39/month when paid access opens. One membership includes the resume builder, cover-letter tools, tracker, extension workflow, and Job Agent. Early access is free by invitation, and there is no paid checkout today. Any later subscription requires your separate agreement.
