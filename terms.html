We grant you a limited, personal, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to install and use the 1stStep.ai Job Agent Chrome extension. In this controlled beta, its intended use is capturing a job listing you are viewing and sending it to the 1stStep.ai Resume Builder or Job Agent review workspace. Host access is limited to app.1ststep.ai and Greenhouse pages at *.greenhouse.io, plus one-time activeTab access when you invoke the extension on another page.

In this beta the extension may:

  • Detect and display job details locally on declared Greenhouse pages while enabled, or read the visible listing or highlighted text on another page after you invoke the extension
  • Let you correct a missing detected title or employer
  • Open the selected job in the 1stStep.ai Resume Builder or Job Agent review workspace
  • Keep a pending local capture handoff for up to 24 hours and, when you have a signed-in account and current Job Agent data consent, save an encrypted captured-job account record for up to 90 days
  • Check your signed-in Job Agent access through an open 1stStep.ai tab

When enabled on a declared Greenhouse page, the extension may detect the active job's URL and listing content locally so it can show its controls. On other pages it reads listing content only after you invoke it. Website content is transmitted to 1stStep.ai only after you choose a user-facing Resume Builder or Job Agent capture action. An account-backed captured job is stored server-side only when you are signed in and have current Job Agent data consent; otherwise no durable captured-job account record is created.
