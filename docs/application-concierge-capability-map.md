## Agent-native discovery and job-acquisition intelligence

The concierge now treats discovery sources as leads and employer/ATS requisitions as authority. Public Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and SmartRecruiters feeds are machine-read first; aggregator evidence cannot advance a role to Verified without an active direct-employer Apply path. Greenhouse, Lever, and SmartRecruiters candidates are re-fetched by exact requisition, while Ashby candidates must remain listed in the current version-1 board response. Standard provider hosts and seven observed custom Greenhouse career hosts are allowlisted exactly per employer, with requisition identity required in the URL path or query. Verification evidence is durable, but package creation always re-fetches the exact requisition and rejects closed, changed, or temporarily unverifiable evidence. The local intelligence layer extracts required and preferred qualifications, responsibilities, experience, domain, technology, leadership, education, location, remote, travel, compensation, work-authorization language, and employment type.

Every discovered role is compared locally with confirmed profile evidence. The deterministic score is 0–100 and maps to Exceptional Match (90–100), Strong Match (80–89), Viable Match (70–79), Stretch (60–69), or Reject (below 60). Hard compensation, work-setting, and excluded-employer conflicts cap the result below the application floor. Only roles at 70 or above with no hard disqualifier enter the working pipeline; uncertainty is held for investigation rather than consuming an application slot. Protected traits and optional demographics are not scoring inputs.

Deduplication now combines employer, requisition, title, location, URL, and description similarity. One versioned title-family relevance policy is shared by server discovery and the restored subscriber job-card view: stale `Found` cards are rechecked against the current mission and withheld when off-mission, while Package Ready, submitted, interview, follow-up, and closed history remains visible. The client also fails closed if an upstream response contains an off-mission title and reports that suppression separately from duplicates and the 70-point fit floor. Phrase-aware role matching prevents a shared word from turning an unrelated role into a match; explicit country codes and known foreign regions prevent foreign-only listings from passing as U.S.-remote. Compensation normalization annualizes only explicit hourly/daily/weekly/monthly intervals and otherwise leaves low ambiguous values unknown. Fit scoring structurally redacts candidate and requisition language about protected traits, optional demographics, authorization, citizenship, sponsorship, clearance, export control, criminal history, medical or drug screening, referrals, restrictive agreements, and outside employment before any scored component is computed. Category-only exclusions remain visible as `notRankedSignals`; regression tests require score, component, and matched-evidence invariance when those facts are added. A reusable local hiring-ecosystem record retains employer, ATS, career URL, structured discovery method, relevant departments, last check, and matching requisitions. Acquisition metrics make qualified application → recruiter screen the primary KPI, with recruiter-screen and offer yield available for later source/title/industry allocation. Full accepted-feed supply is reported separately from the bounded exact-verification and fit-scoring sample. The daily number remains an operational target and never lowers the 70-point quality floor.

## Product boundary for this slice

This wave provides encrypted multi-user campaign metadata, applicant vault storage, consent, daily scheduling, durable direct-employer discovery/package execution, a gated employer-form worker queue, a separately gated single-attempt submission queue, and a bounded receipt-verification queue. It does not claim universal job-board coverage or production-live employer execution. Provider provisioning, external evidence, approval, and deployment remain separate gates.

## Contextual managed-browser vertical slice

The subscriber workspace on `/concierge` now restores encrypted tenant-owned application sessions and opens them from the single **Needs You** queue or a simple job card. It shows the exact employer/domain and package version, masked answer references, the current state, and explicit transmission or final-submission permission. Login, OTP, CAPTCHA, and identity steps stay on the employer page and can be acknowledged without returning their values. The older synthetic workflow remains admin-only evidence. Employer execution, the single-attempt submission worker, and the read-only receipt worker exist only behind independent disabled production gates; the supplied environment cannot submit or ingest live receipt evidence.

The durable state machine covers Discover & Verify → Deduplicate → Tailor Package → Create or Reuse Account/Profile → Autofill → Review Exception → Transmit → Submit → Verify Receipt. It pauses at an employer-specific exception, supports browser-timeout recovery, and never increments the real Submitted count from a simulated receipt. A fail-closed employer-worker contract verifies exact-host navigation, schema-only field extraction, ordinary confirmed-fact references, challenge/credential pauses, consequential questions, no-submit checkpoints, exact-scope single-attempt submission, and receipt-only completion. Production activation still requires the isolated provider, reviewed snapshot and budgets, bounded employer-only egress with DNS/IP controls, ephemeral authenticated handoff, rendered ATS fixtures, recovery drills, employer/ATS terms approval, authoritative receipt capture, and retained signed launch evidence.

Redacted acceptance replay fixtures encode the observed evidence rules:

- Employer candidate-portal history overrides a local-only tracker for Duplicate/In Process decisions.
- An exact direct-employer page overrides stale search snippets and aggregator location descriptions.
- City-scoped “remote” language remains Location Unverified until saved-state eligibility is explicit.
- SuccessFactors sign-in becomes Login Required, followed by action-time Transmission Confirmation Required.
- Invalid employer/title/school identifiers become ATS Configuration Blocked with resumable state.
- Activity status vocabulary includes Verified, Closed by direct page, Location Unverified, Duplicate/In Process, Login Required, Transmission Confirmation Required, ATS Configuration Blocked, and Submitted with authoritative receipt.

Additional replay fixtures cover Jabil `J2457212` and Pratt & Whitney's redacted NPI Buyer as Closed by direct page when Workday no longer exposes the requisitions; Holley Performance Brands `1552` as `Verified - Package Preparation` after direct ADP validation; and Modine `9112` as `Login Required / Retry` while retaining its ATS Configuration Blocked history. Holley's rubber/plastics preference is recorded as a `preferred gap`, while mandatory qualifications use a separate `required gap` field. ADP Continue is modeled as Transmission Confirmation Required because it sends first name, last name, email, mobile number, and phone country to the external ATS.

Batch 32 acceptance behavior adds application-scoped pauses. Modine `9112` becomes `Human Action Required - password/reset` after one explicitly authorized credential attempt returns an invalid-credential response; the form is preserved, further attempts stop, and no reset is triggered silently. After a separately authorized Password Help request transmits the saved email once, the state becomes `Password Reset Requested - Outcome Pending`. The neutral employer response is not evidence that an account exists, a matching account was found, or an email was delivered. Holley `1552` becomes `Human Action Required - latest OTP` after the candidate selects masked email delivery, ADP confirms a verification code was sent, and the `Enter the Verification Code` dialog remains open. The exact pending verification session is preserved when possible. If tab/session cleanup recreates the challenge, the replacement challenge increments a non-secret generation marker, the prior generation is labeled `unusable`, and only the newest OTP action remains open. No code value is accepted by the state API, logged, displayed, or repeatedly retried. Both pipeline roles remain Package Ready, neither becomes Submitted, and another application session can continue while one is suspended. Named batch authorization records that verified profile fields and an approved resume version may be transmitted, but explicitly excludes identity verification, invalid credentials, OTPs, CAPTCHAs, and new employer-specific consent.

After Modine authentication recovery, the post-authentication form-review model preserves the draft and attached resume, marks the full form inspected, and queues only unresolved items. A semantically matching ordinary privacy notice is classified separately and may resolve through its saved standing policy. Newly surfaced material items are explicitly `not-covered` by the prior batch: exact address, exact employment dates, family-at-employer facts, background/personal/employment/education verification, consumer-reporting checks, international internal sharing, pre- and during-employment drug testing, and the at-will/e-signature certification. The checkpoint becomes `Human Action Required - form review`; no role becomes Submitted until an authoritative final application receipt is recorded. The inspection API rejects passwords, OTPs, CAPTCHA values, tokens, resume text, and uploaded document contents.

All candidate values in visible workspace fixtures and timeline events are masked. Passwords, OTPs, CAPTCHA responses, tokens, and receipt identifiers are never accepted or retained by the simulated session.
