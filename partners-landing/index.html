Beta partner terms

Readable program terms

  • Partner payouts are paused during the free controlled beta; beta signups are not commissionable.
  • Planned paid-launch commission: 30% of eligible subscription payments.
  • Planned commission duration: recurring while the eligible referred customer remains actively paid.
  • Planned attribution window: 60 days from the referred visitor's first tracked visit.
  • Planned Job Agent paid launch price: $39/month.
  • Minimum payout threshold: $50.
  • Planned payouts: monthly by PayPal after a 30-day payment confirmation period.
  • A partner-facing referral ledger is planned; the current beta records referral codes for review.
  • An audience discount may be offered at paid launch and will be published before it is active.
  • Applies only to eligible new paid Job Agent customers after checkout opens.
  • User must remain actively subscribed.
  • Refunds and chargebacks are not commissionable.
  • Self-referrals are not allowed.
  • Duplicate accounts, fake signups, spam traffic, and fraudulent referrals are not eligible.
  • 1stStep.ai may review, reject, or adjust referrals that violate program rules.
  • Program terms may change during beta.
Questions, answered

Partner program FAQ

Can I earn commission during the free beta?

No. Your referral code can be recorded now, but beta signups do not accrue commission and no payouts are active.

Do I need a large audience?

No. Relevance and trust matter more than reach. The program is designed for individual coaches and writers as well as larger communities.

How will paid referrals be attributed?

Your current link carries a unique referral code. A 60-day attribution window and partner ledger are planned for paid launch; final tracking rules will be published before commissions begin.

Can I send automated direct messages?

No. The audience finder opens public searches and the local queue drafts messages. You must review and send outreach yourself, and you should follow each platform's rules.

When will payouts start?

Only after paid Job Agent checkout opens. The planned cycle is monthly after a 30-day payment confirmation period and a $50 minimum balance.
