02 / Product facts

A clear record of what the Job Agent does.

Useful details for people and search systems checking the product.

What does 1stStep.ai do?
It checks whether roles are still open, prepares job-specific résumés and cover letters from facts the user confirms, and organizes the work needed for each application.
Does it submit automatically?
No. The applicant reviews the materials and handles the final employer submission. Sign-in, security codes, CAPTCHAs, sensitive questions, attestations, and signatures remain with the user.
What does the extension do?
On supported Greenhouse pages, it can review a role and fill ordinary application fields after the user approves them. It does not store employer passwords or press the final submit button.
