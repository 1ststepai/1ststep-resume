Quick answers

What is the 1stStep.ai Job Agent?

What does 1stStep.ai do?
It checks whether roles are still open, prepares job-specific resumes and cover letters from facts the user confirms, and organizes the work needed for each application.
Does it submit job applications automatically?
No. The applicant reviews the materials and handles the final employer submission. Sign-in, security codes, CAPTCHAs, sensitive questions, attestations, and signatures remain with the user.
What does the Chrome extension do?
On supported Greenhouse pages, it can review the role and fill ordinary application fields after the user chooses and approves them. It does not store employer passwords or press the final submit button.
Where is the official product?
The canonical product website is app.1ststep.ai, and the Job Agent starts at app.1ststep.ai/concierge.
